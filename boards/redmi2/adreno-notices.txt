Adreno native display support

Linux initialization and memory-protection sequences are adapted from Linux v6.12:
 drivers/gpu/drm/msm/adreno/a3xx_gpu.c
 drivers/clk/qcom/gcc-msm8916.c
 drivers/iommu/arm/arm-smmu/qcom_iommu.c

A3xx 32-bit memory-wait packet semantics follow Qualcomm KGSL adreno.h:
https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/msm/+/bcd7b3e3790eb8156580c1b48f545a8a986b6bcd/drivers/gpu/msm/adreno.h
Copyright (c) The Linux Foundation. GPL-2.0-only.

Compute register setup and instruction encodings are derived from
https://github.com/freedreno/freedreno (fdre-a3xx and includes/instr-a3xx.h).
